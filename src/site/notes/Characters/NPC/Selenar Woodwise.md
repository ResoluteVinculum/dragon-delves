Elf **Noble**

Selenar was among the elf warriors in [[Death-at-Sunset\|Death-at-Sunset]]'s lair when the ancient green dragon died, and he is convinced that the new corruption of the wood is not related to dragons. He holds a deep reverence for nature and is anxious for the arriving adventurers to identify and end the threat to the village.
