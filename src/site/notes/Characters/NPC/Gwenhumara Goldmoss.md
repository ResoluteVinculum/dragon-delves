> **Gwenhumara Goldmoss** is an elf healer who tended to the wounded throughout the battle with [[Death-at-Sunset\|Death-at-Sunset]]. 
> She worries that the dragon's vile spirit lingers among the roots of the redwoods.

Gwenhumara happily offers healing services to those in need, but is too scared to leave the village.
