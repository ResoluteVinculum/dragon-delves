Kaynen has made the **Dryad's Tree** his home. He did so just before [[Sunset-is-Nigh\|Sunset-is-Nigh]] was hatched. He pleads for the help with the forest, but only after being initially hostile.

He stated that he had heard rumors about a dragon wyrmling lurking in the lair of [[Death-at-Sunset\|Death-at-Sunset]], and is likely responsible for the poisoning of the wood.
