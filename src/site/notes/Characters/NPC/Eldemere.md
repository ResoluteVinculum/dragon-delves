> Eldemere was once a disciple of the Brass Dragon [[Characters/NPC/Cozmioko\|Cozmioko]]. Shortly after revealing [[Anthradusk\|Anthradusk's]] plans to bring about a second [[Setting/Azure Dominion\|Azure Dominion]] by reviving the [[Setting/Organizations/Blue Dawn\|Blue Dawn]]. Eldemere, ever dedicated began investigating her master's disappearance: discovering the site where Anthradusk slew her. At this hallowed site, Eldemere discovered the [[Setting/Brazen Egg\|Brazen Egg]].
