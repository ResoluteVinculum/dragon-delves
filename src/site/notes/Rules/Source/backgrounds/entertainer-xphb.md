# Entertainer
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 180*  
![/Rules/Source/backgrounds/img/entertainer.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/backgrounds/img/entertainer.webp)

- **Ability Scores.** Strength, Dexterity, Charisma  
- **Feat.** [[Rules/Source/feats/musician-xphb\|Musician]]  
- **Skill Proficiencies.** [[Rules/Source/skills#Acrobatics\|Acrobatics]], [[Rules/Source/skills#Performance\|Performance]]  
- **Tool Proficiency.** Choose one kind of [[Rules/Source/items/musical-instrument-xphb\|Musical Instrument]]  
- **Equipment.** Choose A or B: (A) [[Rules/Source/items/musical-instrument-xphb\|Musical Instrument]] (same as above), [[Rules/Source/items/costume-xphb\|2 Costumes]], [[Rules/Source/items/mirror-xphb\|Mirror]], [[Rules/Source/items/perfume-xphb\|Perfume]], [[Rules/Source/items/travelers-clothes-xphb\|Traveler's Clothes]], 11 GP; or (B) 50 GP  

You spent much of your youth following roving fairs and carnivals, performing odd jobs for musicians and acrobats in exchange for lessons. You may have learned how to walk a tightrope, how to play a [[Rules/Source/items/lute-xphb\|lute]] in a distinct style, or how to recite poetry with impeccable diction. To this day, you thrive on applause and long for the stage.
