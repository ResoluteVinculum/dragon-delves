# Artisan
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 179*  
![/Rules/Source/backgrounds/img/artisan.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/backgrounds/img/artisan.webp)

- **Ability Scores.** Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence  
- **Feat.** [[Rules/Source/feats/crafter-xphb\|Crafter]]  
- **Skill Proficiencies.** [[Rules/Source/skills#Investigation\|Investigation]], [[Rules/Source/skills#Persuasion\|Persuasion]]  
- **Tool Proficiency.** Choose one kind of [[Rules/Source/items/artisans-tools-xphb\|Artisan's Tools]]  
- **Equipment.** Choose A or B: (A) [[Rules/Source/items/artisans-tools-xphb\|Artisan's Tools]] (same as above), 2 [[Rules/Source/items/pouch-xphb\|Pouches]], [[Rules/Source/items/travelers-clothes-xphb\|Traveler's Clothes]], 32 GP; or (B) 50 GP  

You began mopping floors and scrubbing counters in an artisan's workshop for a few coppers per day as soon as you were strong enough to carry a bucket. When you were old enough to apprentice, you learned to create basic crafts of your own, as well as how to sweet-talk the occasional demanding customer. Your trade has also given you a keen eye for detail.
