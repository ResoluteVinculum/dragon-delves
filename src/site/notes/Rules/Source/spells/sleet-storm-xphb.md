# Sleet Storm
*3rd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a miniature umbrella)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Until the spell ends, sleet falls in a 40-foot-tall, 20-foot-radius [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/cylinder-area-of-effect-xphb\|Cylinder]] centered on a point you choose within range. The area is [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/heavily-obscured-xphb\|Heavily Obscured]], and exposed flames in the area are doused.

Ground in the [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/cylinder-area-of-effect-xphb\|Cylinder]] is [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/difficult-terrain-xphb\|Difficult Terrain]]. When a creature enters the [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/cylinder-area-of-effect-xphb\|Cylinder]] for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or have the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Prone\|Prone]] condition and lose [[Rules/Source/conditions#Concentration\|Concentration]].
