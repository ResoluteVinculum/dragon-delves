# Regenerate
*7th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a prayer wheel)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

A creature you touch regains `4d8 + 15` [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]]. For the duration, the target regains 1 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Point]] at the start of each of its turns, and any severed body parts regrow after 2 minutes.

**Classes**: [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-bard\|Bard]]; [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric\|Cleric]]; [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-druid\|Druid]]; [Sorcerer (Divine Soul, Good)](/Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-divine-soul-xge.md "subclass=XGE;class=XPHB")
