# Protection from Poison
*2nd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a creature and end the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition on it. For the duration, the target has [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/advantage-xphb\|Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition, and it has [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/resistance-xphb\|Resistance]] to Poison damage.
