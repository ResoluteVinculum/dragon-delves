# Summon Construct
*4th-level, Conjuration*  
![/Rules/Source/spells/img/summon-construct.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/spells/img/summon-construct.webp)

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a lockbox worth 400+ GP)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You call forth the spirit of a Construct. It manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within range and uses the [[Rules/Source/bestiary/construct/construct-spirit-xphb\|Construct Spirit]] stat block. When you cast the spell, choose a material: Clay, Metal, or Stone. The creature resembles an animate statue (you determine the appearance) made of the chosen material, which determines certain details in its stat block. The creature disappears when it drops to 0 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]] or when the spell ends.

The creature is an ally to you and your allies. In combat, the creature shares your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/initiative-xphb\|Initiative]] count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don't issue any, it takes the [[Rules/Source/actions#Dodge\|Dodge]] action and uses its movement to avoid danger.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** Use the spell slot's level for the spell's level in the stat block.
