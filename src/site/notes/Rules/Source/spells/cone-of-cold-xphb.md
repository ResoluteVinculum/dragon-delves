# Cone of Cold
*5th-level, Evocation*  
![/Rules/Source/spells/img/cone-of-cold.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/spells/img/cone-of-cold.webp)

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a small crystal or glass cone)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a blast of cold air. Each creature in a 60-foot [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/cone-area-of-effect-xphb\|Cone]] originating from you makes a Constitution saving throw, taking `8d8` Cold damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature killed by this spell becomes a frozen statue until it thaws.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 5.

**Classes**: [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-artillerist-tce\|Artificer (Artillerist)]]; [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-wizard\|Wizard]]

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 253. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
