# Aura of Life
*4th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/emanation-area-of-effect-xphb\|Emanation]] for the duration. While in the aura, you and your allies have [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/resistance-xphb\|Resistance]] to Necrotic damage, and your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Point]] maximums can't be reduced. If an ally with 0 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]] starts its turn in the aura, that ally regains 1 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Point]].
