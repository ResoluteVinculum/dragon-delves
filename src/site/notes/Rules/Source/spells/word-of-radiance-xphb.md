# Word of Radiance
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (5-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V, M (a sunburst token)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Burning radiance erupts from you in a 5-foot [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/emanation-area-of-effect-xphb\|Emanation]]. Each creature of your choice that you can see in it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `1d6` Radiant damage.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by `1d6` when you reach levels 5 (`2d6`), 11 (`3d6`), and 17 (`4d6`).

**Classes**: [Bard (College of Lore)](/Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-college-of-lore-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB"); [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric\|Cleric]]; [Sorcerer (Divine Soul, Good)](/Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-divine-soul-xge.md "subclass=XGE;class=XPHB")
