# Aura of Purity
*4th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/emanation-area-of-effect-xphb\|Emanation]] for the duration. While in the aura, you and your allies have [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/resistance-xphb\|Resistance]] to Poison damage and [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/advantage-xphb\|Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end effects that include the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Blinded\|Blinded]], [[Rules/Source/conditions#Charmed\|Charmed]], [[Rules/Source/conditions#Deafened\|Deafened]], [[Rules/Source/conditions#Frightened\|Frightened]], [[Rules/Source/conditions#Paralyzed\|Paralyzed]], [[Rules/Source/conditions#Poisoned\|Poisoned]], or [[Rules/Source/conditions#Stunned\|Stunned]] condition.
