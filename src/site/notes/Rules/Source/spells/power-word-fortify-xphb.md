# Power Word Fortify
*7th-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You fortify up to six creatures you can see within range. The spell bestows 120 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/temporary-hit-points-xphb\|Temporary Hit Points]], which you divide among the spell's recipients.

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 306*
