# Arms of Hadar
*1st-level, Conjuration*  
![/Rules/Source/spells/img/arms-of-hadar.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/spells/img/arms-of-hadar.webp)

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (10-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Invoking Hadar, you cause tendrils to erupt from yourself. Each creature in a 10-foot [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/emanation-area-of-effect-xphb\|Emanation]] originating from you makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes `2d6` Necrotic damage and can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes**: [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-aberrant-sorcery-xphb\|Sorcerer (Aberrant Sorcery)]]; [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-warlock\|Warlock]]

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 243*
