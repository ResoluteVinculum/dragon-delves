# Freedom of Movement
*4th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a leather strap)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target's movement is unaffected by [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/difficult-terrain-xphb\|Difficult Terrain]], and spells and other magical effects can neither reduce the target's [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/speed-xphb\|Speed]] nor cause the target to have the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Paralyzed\|Paralyzed]] or [[Rules/Source/conditions#Restrained\|Restrained]] conditions. The target also has a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/swim-speed-xphb\|Swim Speed]] equal to its [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/speed-xphb\|Speed]].

In addition, the target can spend 5 feet of movement to automatically escape from nonmagical restraints, such as manacles or a creature imposing the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Grappled\|Grappled]] condition on it.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.
