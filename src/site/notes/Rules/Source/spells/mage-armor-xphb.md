# Mage Armor
*1st-level, Abjuration*  
![/Rules/Source/spells/img/mage-armor.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/spells/img/mage-armor.webp)

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a piece of cured leather)
- **Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a willing creature who isn't wearing armor. Until the spell ends, the target's base AC becomes 13 plus its Dexterity modifier. The spell ends early if the target dons armor.

**Classes**: [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-college-of-lore-xphb\|Bard (College of Lore)]]; [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-wizard\|Wizard]]

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 293. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
