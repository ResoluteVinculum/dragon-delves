# Heal
*6th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a creature that you can see within range. Positive energy washes through the target, restoring 70 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]]. This spell also ends the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Blinded\|Blinded]], [[Rules/Source/conditions#Deafened\|Deafened]], and [[Rules/Source/conditions#Poisoned\|Poisoned]] conditions on the target.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The healing increases by `10` for each spell slot level above 6.
