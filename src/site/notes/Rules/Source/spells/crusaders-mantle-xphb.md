# Crusader's Mantle
*3rd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You radiate a magical aura in a 30-foot [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/emanation-area-of-effect-xphb\|Emanation]]. While in the aura, you and your allies each deal an extra `1d4` Radiant damage when hitting with a weapon or an [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb\|Unarmed Strike]].

**Classes**: [Cleric (War Domain)](/Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-war-domain-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB"); [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-paladin\|Paladin]]

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 259*
