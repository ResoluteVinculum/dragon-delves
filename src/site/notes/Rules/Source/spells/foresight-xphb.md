# Foresight
*9th-level, Divination*  


- **Casting time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a hummingbird feather)
- **Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a willing creature and bestow a limited ability to see into the immediate future. For the duration, the target has [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/advantage-xphb\|Advantage]] on [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/d20-test-xphb\|D20 Tests]], and other creatures have [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/disadvantage-xphb\|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against it. The spell ends early if you cast it again.
