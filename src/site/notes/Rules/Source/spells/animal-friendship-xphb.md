# Animal Friendship
*1st-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a morsel of food)
- **Duration:** 24 hours

Target a Beast that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Charmed\|Charmed]] condition for the duration. If you or one of your allies deals damage to the target, the spell ends.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional Beast for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes**: [Bard (College of Lore)](/Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-college-of-lore-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB"); [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-bard\|Bard]]; [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-druid\|Druid]]; [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-ranger\|Ranger]]
