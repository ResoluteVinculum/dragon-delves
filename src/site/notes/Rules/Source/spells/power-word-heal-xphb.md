# Power Word Heal
*9th-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A wave of healing energy washes over one creature you can see within range. The target regains all its [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]]. If the creature has the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Charmed\|Charmed]], [[Rules/Source/conditions#Frightened\|Frightened]], [[Rules/Source/conditions#Paralyzed\|Paralyzed]], [[Rules/Source/conditions#Poisoned\|Poisoned]], or [[Rules/Source/conditions#Stunned\|Stunned]] condition, the condition ends. If the creature has the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Prone\|Prone]] condition, it can use its [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/reaction-xphb\|Reaction]] to stand up.

**Classes**: [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-bard\|Bard]]; [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric\|Cleric]]; [Sorcerer (Divine Soul, Good)](/Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-divine-soul-xge.md "subclass=XGE;class=XPHB")
