# Mass Heal
*9th-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A flood of healing energy flows from you into creatures around you. You restore up to 700 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]], divided as you choose among any number of creatures that you can see within range. Creatures healed by this spell also have the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Blinded\|Blinded]], [[Rules/Source/conditions#Deafened\|Deafened]], and [[Rules/Source/conditions#Poisoned\|Poisoned]] conditions removed from them.

**Classes**: [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-bard\|Bard]]; [[Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric\|Cleric]]; [Sorcerer (Divine Soul, Good)](/Rules/Source/lists/list-spells-classes-divine-soul-xge.md "subclass=XGE;class=XPHB")
