# Feign Death
*3rd-level, Necromancy (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of graveyard dirt)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature and put it into a cataleptic state that is indistinguishable from death.

For the duration, the target appears dead to outward inspection and to spells used to determine the target's status. The target has the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Blinded\|Blinded]] and [[Rules/Source/conditions#Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] conditions, and its [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/speed-xphb\|Speed]] is 0.

The target also has [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/resistance-xphb\|Resistance]] to all damage except Psychic damage, and it has [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/immunity-xphb\|Immunity]] to the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition.
