# Calm Emotions
*2nd-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Each Humanoid in a 20-foot-radius [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/sphere-area-of-effect-xphb\|Sphere]] centered on a point you choose within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or be affected by one of the following effects (choose for each creature):

- The creature has [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/immunity-xphb\|Immunity]] to the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Charmed\|Charmed]] and [[Rules/Source/conditions#Frightened\|Frightened]] conditions until the spell ends. If the creature was already [[Rules/Source/conditions#Charmed\|Charmed]] or [[Rules/Source/conditions#Frightened\|Frightened]], those conditions are suppressed for the duration.  
- The creature becomes [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/indifferent-attitude-xphb\|Indifferent]] about creatures of your choice that it's [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hostile-attitude-xphb\|Hostile]] toward. This indifference ends if the target takes damage or witnesses its allies taking damage. When the spell ends, the creature's attitude returns to normal.
