# Gift of the Depths
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 156*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5

You can breathe underwater, and you gain a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/swim-speed-xphb\|Swim Speed]] equal to your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/speed-xphb\|Speed]].

You can also cast [[Rules/Source/spells/water-breathing-xphb\|Water Breathing]] once without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast it in this way again when you finish a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/long-rest-xphb\|Long Rest]].
