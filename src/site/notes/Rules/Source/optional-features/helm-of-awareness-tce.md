# Helm of Awareness
*Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything p. 21*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 10, A helmet (requires attunement)

While wearing this helmet, a creature has advantage on initiative rolls. In addition, the wearer can't be [[Rules/Source/conditions#Surprised\|surprised]], provided it isn't [[Rules/Source/conditions#Incapacitated\|incapacitated]].
