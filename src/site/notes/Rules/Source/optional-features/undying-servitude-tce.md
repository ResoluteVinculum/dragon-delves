# Undying Servitude
*Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything p. 71*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5

You can cast [[Rules/Source/spells/animate-dead-xphb\|animate dead]] without using a spell slot. Once you do so, you can't cast it in this way again until you finish a long rest.
