# Lifedrinker
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 156. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span>*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9, pact of the blade|xphb

Once per turn when you hit a creature with your pact weapon, you can deal an extra `1d6` Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant damage (your choice) to the creature, and you can expend one of your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-point-dice-xphb\|Hit Point Dice]] to roll it and regain a number of [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]] equal to the roll plus your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Point]]).
