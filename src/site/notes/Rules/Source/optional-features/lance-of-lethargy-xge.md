# Lance of Lethargy
*Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything p. 57*  

**Prerequisite**: [[Rules/Source/spells/eldritch-blast-xphb\|eldritch blast]] cantrip

Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with your [[Rules/Source/spells/eldritch-blast-xphb\|eldritch blast]], you can reduce that creature's speed by 10 feet until the end of your next turn.
