# Trip Attack
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 95*  

When you hit a creature with an attack roll using a weapon or an [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb\|Unarmed Strike]], you can expend one Superiority Die and add the die to the attack's damage roll. If the target is Large or smaller, it must succeed on a Strength saving throw or have the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Prone\|Prone]] condition.
