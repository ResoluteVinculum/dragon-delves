# Lunging Attack
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 95*  

As a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb\|Bonus Action]], you can expend one Superiority Die and take the [[Rules/Source/actions#Dash\|Dash]] action. If you move at least 5 feet in a straight line immediately before hitting with a melee attack as part of the [[Rules/Source/actions#Attack\|Attack]] action on this turn, you can add the Superiority Die to the attack's damage roll.
