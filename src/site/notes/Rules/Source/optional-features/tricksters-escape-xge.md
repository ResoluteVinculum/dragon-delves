# Trickster's Escape
*Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything p. 57*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 7

You can cast [[Rules/Source/spells/freedom-of-movement-xphb\|freedom of movement]] once on yourself without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest.
