# Grasp of Hadar
*Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything p. 57*  

**Prerequisite**: [[Rules/Source/spells/eldritch-blast-xphb\|eldritch blast]] cantrip

Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with your [[Rules/Source/spells/eldritch-blast-xphb\|eldritch blast]], you can move that creature in a straight line 10 feet closer to you.
