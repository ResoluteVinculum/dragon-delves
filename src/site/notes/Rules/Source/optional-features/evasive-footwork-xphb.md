# Evasive Footwork
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 95*  

As a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb\|Bonus Action]], you can expend one Superiority Die and take the [[Rules/Source/actions#Disengage\|Disengage]] action. You also roll the die and add the number rolled to your AC until the start of your next turn.
