# Smoke Grenade
*Explosive*  


- **Cost**: 50 gp
- **Weight**: 2.0 lbs.

As an action, you can either throw a grenade at a point up to 60 feet away or use a Grenade Launcher to propel the grenade to a point up to 1,000 feet away. The grenade explodes at that point, creating a particular effect in a 20-foot-radius [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/sphere-area-of-effect-xphb\|Sphere]].

The area of the [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/sphere-area-of-effect-xphb\|Sphere]] is [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/heavily-obscured-xphb\|Heavily Obscured]] by smoke for 1 minute. A strong wind (such as the [[Rules/Source/spells/gust-of-wind-xphb\|Gust of Wind]] spell) disperses the smoke.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 73*
