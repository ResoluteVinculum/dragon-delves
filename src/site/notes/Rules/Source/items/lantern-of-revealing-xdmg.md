# Lantern of Revealing
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/lantern-of-revealing.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/lantern-of-revealing.webp)


While lit, this hooded lantern burns for 6 hours on 1 pint of oil, shedding [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bright-light-xphb\|Bright Light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb\|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet. [[Rules/Source/conditions#Invisible\|Invisible]] creatures and objects are visible as long as they are in the lantern's [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bright-light-xphb\|Bright Light]]. You can take a [[Rules/Source/actions#Utilize\|Utilize]] action to lower the hood, reducing the lantern's light to [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb\|Dim Light]] in a 5-foot radius.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 275. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
