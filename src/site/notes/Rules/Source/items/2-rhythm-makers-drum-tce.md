# +2 Rhythm-Maker's Drum
*Wondrous item, instrument ([[Rules/Source/items/drum-xphb\|drum]]), rare (requires attunement by a bard)*  


- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

While holding this [[Rules/Source/items/drum-xphb\|drum]], you gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs of your bard spells.

As an action, you can play the [[Rules/Source/items/drum-xphb\|drum]] to regain one use of your Bardic Inspiration feature. This property of the [[Rules/Source/items/drum-xphb\|drum]] can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything p. 134*
