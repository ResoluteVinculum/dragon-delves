# Mace of Disruption
*Weapon ([[Rules/Source/items/mace-xphb\|mace]]), rare (requires attunement)*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/mace-of-disruption.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/mace-of-disruption.webp)

- **Damage**: 1d6 bludgeoning
- **Weight**: 4.0 lbs.

When you hit a Fiend or an Undead with this magic weapon, that creature takes an extra `2d6` Radiant damage. If the target has 25 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]] or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be destroyed. On a successful save, the creature has the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Frightened\|Frightened]] condition until the end of your next turn.

## Light

While you hold this weapon, it sheds [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bright-light-xphb\|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb\|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 276. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
