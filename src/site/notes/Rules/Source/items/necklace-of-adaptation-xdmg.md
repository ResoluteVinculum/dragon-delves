# Necklace of Adaptation
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/necklace-of-adaptation.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/necklace-of-adaptation.webp)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

While wearing this necklace, you can breathe normally in any environment, and you have [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/advantage-xphb\|Advantage]] on saving throws made to avoid or end the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Poisoned\|Poisoned]] condition.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 280. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
