# Ring of Three Wishes
*Ring, legendary*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/ring-of-three-wishes.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/ring-of-three-wishes.webp)


While wearing this ring, you can expend 1 of its 3 charges to cast [[Rules/Source/spells/wish-xphb\|Wish]] from it. The ring becomes nonmagical when you use the last charge.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 296. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
