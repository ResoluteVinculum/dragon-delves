# Ring of Animal Influence
*Ring, rare*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/ring-of-animal-influence.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/ring-of-animal-influence.webp)


This ring has 3 charges, and it regains `1d3` expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can expend 1 charge to cast one of the following spells (save DC 13) from it:

- [[Rules/Source/spells/animal-friendship-xphb\|Animal Friendship]]  
- [[Rules/Source/spells/fear-xphb\|Fear]] (affects Beasts only)  
- [[Rules/Source/spells/speak-with-animals-xphb\|Speak with Animals]]  

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 292. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
