# Eyes of the Eagle
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  
![/Rules/Source/items/img/eyes-of-the-eagle.webp\|right wmed](/img/user/Rules/Source/items/img/eyes-of-the-eagle.webp)


These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, you have [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/advantage-xphb\|Advantage]] on Wisdom ([[Rules/Source/skills#Perception\|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight. In conditions of clear visibility, you can make out details of even extremely distant creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 261. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
