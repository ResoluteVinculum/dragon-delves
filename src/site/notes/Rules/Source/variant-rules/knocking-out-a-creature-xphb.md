# Knocking Out a Creature
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 370. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

When you would reduce a creature to 0 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]] with a melee attack, you can instead reduce the creature to 1 Hit Point. The creature then has the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Unconscious\|Unconscious]] condition and starts a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/short-rest-xphb\|Short Rest]].

The creature remains Unconscious until it regains any [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]] or until someone uses an action to administer first aid to it, which requires a successful DC 10 Wisdom ([[Rules/Source/skills#Medicine\|Medicine]]) check.
