# Flying
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 367. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

A variety of effects allow a creature to fly. While flying, you fall if you have the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Incapacitated\|Incapacitated]] or [[Rules/Source/conditions#Prone\|Prone]] condition or your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/fly-speed-xphb\|Fly Speed]] is reduced to 0. You can stay aloft in those circumstances if you can hover.
