# Familiars
*Source: Monster Manual p. 347* 

Any spellcaster that can cast the [[Rules/Source/spells/find-familiar-xphb\|find familiar]] spell (such as an apprentice, warlock, or wizard) is likely to have a familiar. The familiar can be one of the creatures described in the spell (see the "Player's Handbook") or some other Tiny monster, such as a [[Rules/Source/bestiary/undead/crawling-claw-xmm\|crawling claw]], an [[Rules/Source/bestiary/fiend/imp-xmm\|imp]], a [[Rules/Source/bestiary/dragon/pseudodragon-xmm\|pseudodragon]], or a [[Rules/Source/bestiary/fiend/quasit-xmm\|quasit]].

> [!note]
> This variant is intended for NPCs. (https://twitter.com/JeremyECrawford/status/920714594334654465)
