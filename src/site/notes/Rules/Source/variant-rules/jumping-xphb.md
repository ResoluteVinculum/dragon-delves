# Jumping
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 370. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

When you jump, you make either a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/long-jump-xphb\|Long Jump]] (horizontal) or a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/high-jump-xphb\|High Jump]] (vertical).
