# Possession
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 372. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

Some effects cause a creature to be possessed by another creature or entity. A possessing effect defines how the possession operates. Possession can be prevented by the [[Rules/Source/spells/protection-from-evil-and-good-xphb\|Protection from Evil and Good]] spell and ended by the [[Rules/Source/spells/dispel-evil-and-good-xphb\|Dispel Evil and Good]] spell.
