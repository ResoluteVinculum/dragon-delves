# Spell Attack
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 374. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

A spell attack is an attack roll made as part of a spell or another [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/magical-effect-xphb\|magical effect]].
