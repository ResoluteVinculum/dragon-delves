# Challenge Rating
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 363. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

Challenge Rating (CR) summarizes the threat a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/monster-xphb\|monster]] poses to a group of four [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/player-character-xphb\|player characters]]. Compare a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/monster-xphb\|monster's]] CR to the characters' level. If the CR is higher, the [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/monster-xphb\|monster]] is likely a danger. If the CR is lower, the [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/monster-xphb\|monster]] likely poses little threat. But circumstances and the number of [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/player-character-xphb\|player characters]] can significantly alter how threatening a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/monster-xphb\|monster]] is in actual play. The "Dungeon Master's Guide" provides guidance to the DM on using CR while planning potential combat [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/encounter-xphb\|encounters]].
