# Gold Dragon

## Regional Effects
_Source: Monster Manual (2024)_

The region containing an adult or ancient gold dragon's lair is altered by its presence, creating the following effects:

- **Dream Messenger.** While in its lair, the dragon can cast [[Rules/Source/spells/dream-xphb\|Dream]], requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability. When casting the spell this way, the dragon can target any creature within 6 miles.  
- **Foretelling Fog.** The area within 1 mile of the lair is [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/lightly-obscured-xphb\|Lightly Obscured]] by opalescent fog. While in that area, creatures can't be [[Rules/Source/conditions#Surprised\|surprised]], as the fog swirls into shapes that warn of danger.  

If the dragon dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
