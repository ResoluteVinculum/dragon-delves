# Ram

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 97*  

A Ram consists of a movable gallery equipped with a heavy log suspended from two roof beams by chains. The log is shod in iron and used to batter through doors and barricades. Positioning a Ram requires three [[Rules/Source/actions#Utilize\|Utilize]] actions. Then a crew member can use the Ram action.

The gallery roof gives the operators [[Rules/Source/tables/cover-xphb\|Total Cover]] against attacks and other effects from above.
