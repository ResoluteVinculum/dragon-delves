# Skill Expert
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 207*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Skill Proficiency.** You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.

**Expertise.** Choose one skill in which you have proficiency but lack [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/expertise-xphb\|Expertise]]. You gain [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/expertise-xphb\|Expertise]] with that skill.
