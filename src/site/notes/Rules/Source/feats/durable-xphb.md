# Durable
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 203*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Defy Death.** You have [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/advantage-xphb\|Advantage]] on [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/death-saving-throw-xphb\|Death Saving Throws]].

**Speedy Recovery.** As a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb\|Bonus Action]], you can expend one of your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-point-dice-xphb\|Hit Point Dice]], roll the die, and regain a number of [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]] equal to the roll.
