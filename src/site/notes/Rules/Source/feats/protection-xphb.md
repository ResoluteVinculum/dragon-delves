# Protection
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 209*  

**Prerequisite**: Fighting Style Feature

When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you that is within 5 feet of you, you can take a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/reaction-xphb\|Reaction]] to interpose your [[Rules/Source/items/shield-xphb\|Shield]] if you're holding one. You impose [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/disadvantage-xphb\|Disadvantage]] on the triggering attack roll and all other attack rolls against the target until the start of your next turn if you remain within 5 feet of the target.
