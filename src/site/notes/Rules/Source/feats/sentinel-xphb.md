# Sentinel
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 207*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th; Strength 13 or higher or 4th; Dexterity 13 or higher

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Guardian.** Immediately after a creature within 5 feet of you takes the [[Rules/Source/actions#Disengage\|Disengage]] action or hits a target other than you with an attack, you can make an [[Rules/Source/actions#Opportunity Attack\|Opportunity Attack]] against that creature.

**Halt.** When you hit a creature with an [[Rules/Source/actions#Opportunity Attack\|Opportunity Attack]], the creature's [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/speed-xphb\|Speed]] becomes 0 for the rest of the current turn.
