# Boon of Recovery
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 211*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Last Stand.** When you would be reduced to 0 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]], you can drop to 1 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Point]] instead and regain a number of [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]] equal to half your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Point]] maximum. Once you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/long-rest-xphb\|Long Rest]].

**Recover Vitality.** You have a pool of ten d10s. As a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb\|Bonus Action]], you can expend dice from the pool, roll those dice, and regain a number of [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]] equal to the roll's total. You regain all the expended dice when you finish a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/long-rest-xphb\|Long Rest]].
