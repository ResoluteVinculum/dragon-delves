# Tough
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 202*  

Your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Point]] maximum increases by an amount equal to twice your character level when you gain this feat. Whenever you gain a character level thereafter, your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Point]] maximum increases by an additional 2 [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]].
