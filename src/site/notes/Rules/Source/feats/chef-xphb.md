# Chef
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 202*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Constitution or Wisdom by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Cook's Utensils.** You gain proficiency with [[Rules/Source/items/cooks-utensils-xphb\|Cook's Utensils]] if you don't already have it.

**Replenishing Meal.** As part of a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/short-rest-xphb\|Short Rest]], you can cook special food if you have ingredients and [[Rules/Source/items/cooks-utensils-xphb\|Cook's Utensils]] on hand. You can prepare enough of this food for a number of creatures equal to 4 plus your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/proficiency-xphb\|Proficiency Bonus]]. At the end of the [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/short-rest-xphb\|Short Rest]], any creature who eats the food and spends one or more Hit Dice to regain [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]] regains an extra `1d8` [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb\|Hit Points]].

**Bolstering Treats.** With 1 hour of work or when you finish a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/long-rest-xphb\|Long Rest]], you can cook a number of treats equal to your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/proficiency-xphb\|Proficiency Bonus]] if you have ingredients and [[Rules/Source/items/cooks-utensils-xphb\|Cook's Utensils]] on hand. These special treats last 8 hours after being made. A creature can use a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb\|Bonus Action]] to eat one of those treats to gain a number of [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/temporary-hit-points-xphb\|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/proficiency-xphb\|Proficiency Bonus]].
