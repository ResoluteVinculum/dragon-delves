# Unarmed Fighting
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 210*  

**Prerequisite**: Fighting Style Feature

When you hit with your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb\|Unarmed Strike]] and deal damage, you can deal Bludgeoning damage equal to `1d6` plus your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage of an [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb\|Unarmed Strike]]. If you aren't holding any weapons or a [[Rules/Source/items/shield-xphb\|Shield]] when you make the attack roll, the `d6` becomes a `d8`.

At the start of each of your turns, you can deal `1d4` Bludgeoning damage to one creature [[Rules/Source/conditions#Grappled\|Grappled]] by you.
