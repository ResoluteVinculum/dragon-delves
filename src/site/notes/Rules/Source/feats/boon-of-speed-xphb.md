# Boon of Speed
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 211*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Escape Artist.** As a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb\|Bonus Action]], you can take the [[Rules/Source/actions#Disengage\|Disengage]] action, which also ends the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Grappled\|Grappled]] condition on you.

**Quickness.** Your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/speed-xphb\|Speed]] increases by 30 feet.
