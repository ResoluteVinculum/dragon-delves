# Athlete
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 202*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th; Strength 13 or higher or 4th; Dexterity 13 or higher

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Climb Speed.** You gain a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/climb-speed-xphb\|Climb Speed]] equal to your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/speed-xphb\|Speed]].

**Hop Up.** When you have the [[Rules/Source/conditions#Prone\|Prone]] condition, you can right yourself with only 5 feet of movement.

**Jumping.** You can make a running [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/long-jump-xphb\|Long]] or [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/high-jump-xphb\|High Jump]] after moving only 5 feet.
