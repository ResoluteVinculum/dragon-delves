# Lucky
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 201*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Luck Points.** You have a number of Luck Points equal to your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/proficiency-xphb\|Proficiency Bonus]] and can spend the points on the benefits below. You regain your expended Luck Points when you finish a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/long-rest-xphb\|Long Rest]].

**Advantage.** When you roll a `d20` for a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/d20-test-xphb\|D20 Test]], you can spend 1 Luck Point to give yourself [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/advantage-xphb\|Advantage]] on the roll.

**Disadvantage.** When a creature rolls a `d20` for an attack roll against you, you can spend 1 Luck Point to impose [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/disadvantage-xphb\|Disadvantage]] on that roll.
