# Squat Nimbleness
*Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything p. 75*  

**Prerequisite**: [[Rules/Source/races/dwarf-xphb\|Dwarf]] or a Small race

You are uncommonly nimble for your race. You gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
- Increase your walking speed by 5 feet.  
- You gain proficiency in the [[Rules/Source/skills#Acrobatics\|Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Source/skills#Athletics\|Athletics]] skill (your choice).  
- You have advantage on any Strength ([[Rules/Source/skills#Athletics\|Athletics]]) or Dexterity ([[Rules/Source/skills#Acrobatics\|Acrobatics]]) check you make to escape from being [[Rules/Source/conditions#Grappled\|grappled]].
