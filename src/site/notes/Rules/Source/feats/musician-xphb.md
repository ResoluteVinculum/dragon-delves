# Musician
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 201*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Instrument Training.** You gain proficiency with three Musical Instruments of your choice.

**Encouraging Song.** As you finish a [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/short-rest-xphb\|Short]] or [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/long-rest-xphb\|Long Rest]], you can play a song on a [[Rules/Source/items/musical-instrument-xphb\|Musical Instrument]] with which you have proficiency and give [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/heroic-inspiration-xphb\|Heroic Inspiration]] to allies who hear the song. The number of allies you can affect in this way equals your [[Rules/Source/variant-rules/proficiency-xphb\|Proficiency Bonus]].
