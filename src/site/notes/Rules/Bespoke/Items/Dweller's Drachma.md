# Dweller's Drachma
*Wondrous item, very-rare*  

This gold coin, on it's head's side, has a depiction of an Ancient Blue Dragon towering over a tiny, wind-wrecked tent intricately enameled in deep blues and muddy browns and on the tails, an extravagant mansion with bronze accents and a community on the lawn surrounding a speaking adult bronze dragon.

The coin has 1 charge and regains its expended charge daily at dawn. You can take a [Magic](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#MagicAction) action to toss the coin, expending its charge. Roll any die to determine whether the coin comes up heads (on an even number) or tails (on an odd number). The roll also determines the effect:

_**Heads.**_ Leomund's Tiny Hut is cast, but a 30ft Sphere of thunderstorm surrounds it. If a creature begins their turn inside the storm, but not in the emanation, they take `2d8` Thunder damage.

_**Tails.**_ A shimmering door appears in range, allowing any the coin flipper select to enter and be transported to a simple sunny glade through an arch that also serves as the exit. The glade, at first, contains one simple structure, a small barn with six comfortable pads made up. After 12 hours or after the caster exits through the archway, the occupants are expelled, except hirelings bound by bastion rules.
