# The Empty Lands
The Sword Coast was once an expansive tract wilderness, dotted with independent city states: otherwise populated by bands of roving monsters. Travelling through the Sword Coast is much like travelling through [[Ohio\|Ohio]], "Why?" 

The Sword Coast likely gets its name from the white cliffs that rise above the coastline between the [[River Dessarin\|River Dessarin]] and [[Baldur’s Gate\|Baldur’s Gate]], but has been theorized to be because of its inhabitants: humanoid, bestial, and monstrous.
## Geography
The exact borders of the Sword Coast are highly contested, but the generally accepted answer describes the southern border as the desert, merchant nation [[Amn\|Amn]] and the northern border as [[Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]]. There is often a distinction made between the [[Sword Coast North\|Sword Coast North]].
