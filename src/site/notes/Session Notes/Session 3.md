# Events
## Curtain Call
The party was escorted by [[Sunny Lightpurse\|Sunny Lightpurse]] to The Dragmire Darlings' showing of **The Hooky-Horror Picture Show**. During the openning song, a scream rang out and a performer ran out screaming about the murder of the troope's leader **Kacarosh**. 

### Investigation
The party immediately jumped into action and began investigation, first via interrogation. Stiss, Stramo, and Ginnish were the primary suspects; after the initial round of questions, Stiss ran away to his caravan and [[Characters/PC/Orrath, The Withered One\|Orrath]] followed. Where he pummeled the cute dog wearing the jester's hat. After kicking the door down and catching Stiss with his pants down (damning documents in hand). Stiss admits to writing fake hatemail about **Kacarosh** and that he is jealous of the attention Stiss' parents given him over their own son. During the interrogation, [[Characters/PC/Vespa Littleflight\|Vespa]] and [[Characters/PC/Coriander Hope\|Coriander]], discovered that the other two also have reasons to hate Kacarosh.

[[Characters/PC/Vespa Littleflight\|Vespa]] snuck off to investigate Kacarosh's caravan, where he found a pristine caravan with animal feed in one desk drawer and an impossible to-do list in the other. Leaving to go search Stramo's caravan to find a smattering of destroyed posters with a red "X" over Kacarosh's face and a weasel that spooked him out of the caravan.  

[[Characters/PC/Coriander Hope\|Coriander]] utilized three simultaneous effects of the [[Rules/rendered-blocks/spells/thaumaturgy-xphb\|Thaumaturgy]] cantrip to intimidate all five satyrs into blabbering about how they all have a reason to want **Kacarosh** dead. Ginnish ran away, throwing down some props to prevent [[Characters/PC/Orrath, The Withered One\|Orrath]] from following. Inside Ginnish's caravan, [[Characters/PC/Vespa Littleflight\|Vespa]] found the murder weapon under the rug: a blade from the **Dr. Hook-n-Slicer** costume. Immediately following this discovery, Ginnish slammed the door behind her, revealing the other blade from the costume.

The party handily fought Ginnish and made the case to the rest of the troupe that **Kacarosh** maybe deserved to die because of homophobia. Found on Ginnish's person, the party took a beautifully decorated [[Rules/rendered-blocks/items/bag-of-holding-xdmg\|Bag of Holding]] and were given 87GP for solving the murder.

Thinking back to the song they only got to hear a little of, the party went back to the caravan to ask some questions. They learned the names are of legendary figures from the Sword Coast's storied past, the most recent being [[Vaerazuun\|Vaerazuun]]: the matriarch of [[Setting/Organizations/Blue Dawn\|Blue Dawn]] and the cause of the centuries long [[Setting/Azure Dominion\|Azure Dominion]] and [[Anthradusk\|Anthradusk]]'s mother.
