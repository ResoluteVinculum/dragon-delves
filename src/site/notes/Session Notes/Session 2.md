# Events
After successfully helping [[Characters/NPC/Gwenhumara Goldmoss\|Gwenhumara]] gather samples and create a powerful antitoxin against the creeping poison of the redwood grove, the party retired for the night. [[Characters/PC/Coriander Hope\|Coriander]] choosing to spend the night with [[Fergus Deerborn\|Fergus]]. 

In the morning, [[Characters/NPC/Gwenhumara Goldmoss\|Gwenhumara]] delivers her 12 vials of Antitoxin and watched the party off to their next task: curing the woods.

Travelling through the redwood grove again, the party went back to [[Characters/NPC/Kaynen\|Kaynen]] to ask if he would be an ally in their upcoming spelunk. He declined, expressing that his purpose is to guard **HIS TREE**. 

Progressing to the cave, the party was immediately confronted by [[Armin Whisperwind\|Armin Whisperwind]]: intent on protecting the evil within. Armin reveals that the poison is emanating from a *Green Dragon Wyrmling* named [[Sunset-is-Nigh\|Sunset-is-Nigh]], utilizing the latent power within his mother's ([[Death-at-Sunset\|Death-at-Sunset]]) corpse. Initially combative, the party was able to calm Armin and find out that [[Sunset-is-Nigh\|Sunset-is-Nigh]] has threatened the lives of the entire village if he abandons his post.

Upon entering the cave, the party was afronted with a recently familiar site: elves manipulated by the cruelty of a *Green Dragon*. The other missing elves **Darrin**, **Jherris**, **Lyrienne**, and **Rayna**. [[Sunset-is-Nigh\|Sunset-is-Nigh]] has once again convinced that he holds an immense amount of power, threatening their lives via the power of *his mother's ghost*. Being very convincing, yet again, the party convinced the tormented elves that they are safe and sent them to escort Armin back to [[Redwood Watch\|Redwood Watch]]. 

The party progressed deeper into the cave, drawn by the sounds of claws on bone. [[Characters/PC/Orrath, The Withered One\|Orrath]] decided to approach the fetid cloud obscuring the large *Adult Green Dragon* corpse causing [[Sunset-is-Nigh\|Sunset-is-Nigh]] to attack!

During combat, one of [[Sunset-is-Nigh\|Sunset-is-Nigh]]'s servants, [[Marilissa\|Marilissa]] joined the fight. She brought a *Giant Constrictor Snake* and many other minor snakes and creatures. The combat went quickly, with [[Death-at-Sunset\|Death-at-Sunset]] nearly eliminating all three adventurers with his poison breath weapon.

The party (please come up with a name) successfully defeated [[Sunset-is-Nigh\|Sunset-is-Nigh]] in the cave. Avoiding much potential combat and narrowly defeating [[Marilissa\|Marilissa]] in time to scare her snakes away. The team acquired the young dragon's meager hoard and made way to [[Redwood Watch\|Redwood Watch]]. Arriving with the rescued elves and [[Armin Whisperwind\|Armin]], the village begain organizing a celebration for [[Characters/PC/Orrath, The Withered One\|Orrath]], [[Characters/PC/Coriander Hope\|Coriander]], and [[Characters/PC/Vespa Littleflight\|Vespa]]'s heroic deeds. While the most important elves in town (for party prep, at least) humored Coriander and Orrath's sexual advances, they managed to remain focused enough to create a boisterous borough bash. All while Vespa snuck off to the do the "dirty" deed.

During the party, the Brazen Egg seemed to influence the party's attention. A second became an eternity when "The Baker's Dawn", a nonsense song, played. The party felt compelled to find a baker, but were informed that the nearest bakery was at the southern edge of the forest, east of the **Cragmaw Castle**.
