# Events
[[Characters/PC/Coriander Hope\|Coriander Hope]], [[Characters/PC/Orrath, The Withered One\|Orrath, The Withered One]], and [[Characters/PC/Vespa Littleflight\|Vespa Littleflight]] meet on a road outside of what was once the [[Greenfields\|Greenfields]] in the [[Western Heartlands\|Western Heartlands]]. [[Characters/PC/Coriander Hope\|Coriander]] fleeing the [[Setting/Haskasori\|Sandstone City]] on the southern border, [[Characters/PC/Orrath, The Withered One\|Orrath]] wandering the desert due to his curse, and [[Characters/PC/Vespa Littleflight\|Vespa]] seeking customers for their wares.

This unlikely crew seemed drawn together in this moment to answer a cry for help as a tall human woman is being robbed by a group of bandits. The party, choosing intimidation over violence, successfully stopped the bandits. 

[[Characters/NPC/Eldemere\|Eldemere]] introduced herself to the party, revealing herself to be a disciple of [[Characters/NPC/Cozmioko\|Cozmioko]] and bearing the news that the Ancient Brass Dragon has been defeated by [[Anthradusk\|Anthradusk]]. [[Anthradusk\|Anthradusk]] seeks to revive the [[Setting/Organizations/Blue Dawn\|Blue Dawn]]: his ancestors responsible for the [[Setting/Azure Dominion\|Azure Dominion]], which the world has only just begun to recover from.

[[Characters/PC/Orrath, The Withered One\|Orrath]] feeling his curse's compulsion to stay within the desert be lifted, deems the [[Setting/Brazen Egg\|Brazen Egg]] a necessity. A necessity which [[Characters/NPC/Eldemere\|Eldemere]] informs the party that has the potential to stop [[Anthradusk\|Anthradusk's]] plans. The [[Setting/Brazen Egg\|Brazen Egg]] must be steeped in the hoards of ten dragons to unlock its power.

The [[Setting/Brazen Egg\|Brazen Egg]]  compelled the party to rest, granting [[Characters/PC/Orrath, The Withered One\|Orrath]] with a vision of six great redwoords geometrically growing in a grove amidst the woods. Knowing of [[Redwood Watch\|Redwood Watch]] in the [[Neverwinter Wood\|Neverwinter Wood]], the party set about their long jaunt into the north, opting to travel through the familiar and "safe" [[Setting/Sword Coast\|Sword Coast]] using a sea faring vessel.



# Story Beats
Upon arrival to [[Redwood Watch\|Redwood Watch]], the party were approached by two council members, [[Characters/NPC/Selenar Woodwise\|Selenar Woodwise]] and [[Characters/NPC/Gwenhumara Goldmoss\|Gwenhumara Goldmoss]], of the village believing they are heroes answering their call. [[Characters/PC - Private/Vespa Littleflight - Private\|Vespa]] lied and said "Yes". The council asks them to help find the missing villagers and the missing councilman, [[Armin Whisperwind\|Armin Whisperwind]]. As the council and adventurers parted ways, [[Characters/NPC/Gwenhumara Goldmoss\|Gwenhumara]] asked that the party collect samples from each of the great redwoods in the grove.

The party successfully collected all of the required samples and Gwenhumara informed them that in the morning, she will be able to produce a ridiculous amount of antitoxin against the poison of the woods. Having forgotten about the missing villagers, the party decided to utilize the free room they were offered and stay in a room above [[Fergus Deerborn\|Fergus]]'s Grill. [[Characters/PC/Coriander Hope\|Coriander]] decided to stay in Fergus' bed and enjoy the night with the grillmaster himself.
